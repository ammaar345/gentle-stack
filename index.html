MM2 🔪 LEVELING

Murder Mystery 2 — Level Up Fast

Every second alive earns XP. Here's how to maximize it

Updated August 2026

THE SYSTEM

How XP Actually Works

Most players waste rounds collecting coins and hit level caps without ranking up. Here's the mechanic they miss

  • XP comes from survival time and role actions — not coins. Coins cap at 50 per round and buy cosmetics only
  • Win rounds consistently Every round completed adds XP passively based on how long you stayed alive
  • Role matters Innocents earn for survival. Sheriffs earn for stopping the murderer. Murderers earn for kills
  • Coins ≠ XP You could collect max coins every round and never gain a single level if you die in the first 10 seconds
Core rule Survival time is the #1 XP multiplier. Play to stay alive, not to score kills. Every extra 30 seconds in a round is extra XP banked.

POSITIONING

Map Picks That Accelerate XP

The map you vote for directly changes how fast you level. Pick the right one for your current goal

🔫 Combat Maps — Fast Rounds

  • House 2, Office 2 — small, aggressive
  • Best for Sheriff XP quick-flips
  • Rounds end fast — for shorter sessions

⚖️ Balanced Maps — Steady XP

  • Biolab, Hospital 3 — mid-sized layouts
  • Good hiding spots + manageable kill chases
  • Easy to pick up consistently

🕵️ Large Maps — Max Survival

  • Factory, Mansion 2 — sprawling layouts
  • Longest survival time per round
  • Best for Innocent XP grinds

🎯 Story Maps — Varied

  • Unique layouts with objectives
  • Distract the murderer by completing tasks
  • Survival XP stacks while the killer is busy

ROLE PLAY

Innocent · Sheriff · Murderer

😇 Innocent

Survival = XP. Speed kills your rank

  • Rotate hiding spots — repeat one trick makes you predictable
  • Stay near groups but don't cluster at the choke
  • Call suspicious behavior early and clearly
  • Vote for maps with lots of hiding options
Best map: Mansion 2 or Factory — hide, listen, call out

👮 Sheriff

One clean shot = big XP. Rushing = wasted round

  • Landing the shot on the murderer gives a huge XP boost
  • Patience wins — bait the murderer into making a move
  • Use psychological tricks: fake hesitation
  • Practice aim in custom lobbies
Best map: Office 2 or House 2 — tight corridors = guaranteed sightlines

💀 Murderer

Patience > rushing. Each slow kill = more rounds = more XP

  • Watch the map — isolate players who wander alone
  • Longer round = more total XP from all your kills
  • Use psychological play: make Innocents doubt each other
  • Sabotage (if available) creates chaos — hunt during the distraction
Best map: Biolab — enough cover to stalk, enough structure to separate groups

ADVANCED

Pro Grinding Tips

  1. Play with friends Communication extends round survival by a huge margin. Call out the murderer's position and coordinate body-checking
  2. Switch roles every few rounds Staying alive as Innocent builds XP, but a Sheriff round is a jackpot. Mix it up
  3. Don't burn out Grinding XP is easier when you're enjoying it. Take breaks — steady sessions outperform marathon burns
  4. Use cosmetics as bait Rare items make players cautious or distracted — use that tension to slip past them
  5. Focus on "smart plays" over aggressive plays A good surviving Innocent who never kills anyone will outrank an aggressive Murderer who gets shot in round one.

Open eldorado.gg/become-seller, create the account, and start ID verification

FEES vs ELDORADO / G2G

Seller Fee Guide: Eldorado.gg vs G2G.com

The full cost of selling on each platform — commission, withdrawal, KYC, escrow, hidden rules

Updated 22 Aug 2026

TL;DR

Eldorado.gg

Best for currency sellers, top-up sellers, and anyone who wants instant withdrawal. 30% commission on GTA 5 accounts, 20% on COD/Fortnite/R6. 0% on gift cards and top-ups is unbeatable

~1.5–30% commission · instant to 5-day payouts

G2G.com

Best for account sellers who grind Legendary rank (7.99% vs Eldorado's 10%). 14-day account hold is the biggest pain. Wider game catalog. PayPal option attracts more buyers — but you get PayPal-charged too

4.99–12.99% commission · 15 min–14 day credit

Both operate the same way — buyer pays the marketplace, marketplace holds money in escrow until the seller delivers, marketplace takes commission, seller gets the rest minus withdrawal fees.

VERDICT

Pick your platform

Pick Eldorado if…

  • You sell currency, top-ups, gift cards, or OSRS gold (0% / 1.5% — industry leaders)

  • You want the buyer trust of a 4.4–4.5★ Trustpilot at huge volume

PRICING

Pricing tiers that actually convert

  1. Tier 1 — Impulse ($1–$5) SaB named brainrots, MM2 cheap godlys, Blox Fruits perms. Lowest friction, fast turnover
  2. Tier 2 — Considered ($10–$40) Hand-leveled LoL ranked accounts, MM2 chromas, named plants / pets with neon + potion
  3. Tier 3 — Big ticket ($50+) Fortnite OG / stacked, Valorant skin + rank, ranked GM+ accounts. Highest fee tiers — check commission math first
  4. Tier 4 — Knowledge Guides, spreadsheets, SOP packs. Near-0% marginal cost. Highest gross margin if traffic is there
Fee math rule of thumb a $1 item at 5% fee = $0.95 net. Anything under $0.50 needs bulk or it's a fee loss. Always price with the platform fee baked in.

PACKAGING & DELIVERY

What makes a listing convert

  • Clear title Max 2 keywords. Name the variant, the platform, the rank, the season. Buyers scan, not read
  • Real screenshots Inventory + email on the same screen. Max 5 photos. Current, not stock
  • Delivery time Don't overpromise. Under-promise and beat it — Instant should mean actually instant
  • Reply speed Items: first reply in 30 minutes or auto-cancel. Account disputes: proof in 12 hours or refunded
  • Stay on-platform All chat, all payment. Off-platform = permaban
Hard rules that get stores banned off-site payment · off-site ads · fake titles · more than 2 keywords · lottery / random loot · under-18 · multi-accounting · insults · late delivery as a habit
